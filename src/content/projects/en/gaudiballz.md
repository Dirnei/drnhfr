Sort until every colour stands on its own: balls move from flask to flask
until nothing is mixed any more. A genre everyone knows — and one that in the
app stores is almost always buried under advertising.

Not here. No account, no ads, no price tag. The page installs onto the home
screen as an app and runs without a connection afterwards. Portrait, one
hand, a few minutes at a time.

Built because it was fun, not because there was a plan behind it.
